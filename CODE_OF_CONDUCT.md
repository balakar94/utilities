# Code of Conduct

This project adopts [Contributor Covenant v3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

- Be brief, kind, and specific in Issues and PRs.
- Harassment, insults, spam, and discriminatory content are not tolerated.
- Respect each tool's license and authorship.

## Reporting

Contact a maintainer privately, or use the security reporting channel for sensitive cases.
Maintainers respond within a reasonable time and may moderate, edit,
or remove content or access depending on severity.

The binding full text is Contributor Covenant v3.0 (link above).
